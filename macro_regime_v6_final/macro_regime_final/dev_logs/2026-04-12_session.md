## Changes Implemented

### 1. Stock concentration: `top_n_stocks_per_sector` 10 → 5

**File:** `config.py`  
**Change:** `top_n_stocks_per_sector: int = 10` → `top_n_stocks_per_sector: int = 5`

**Rationale (first principles):**  
Momentum alpha is theoretically strongest in the top decile of cross-sectional momentum. With 10 stocks out of ~40-80 per sector we're approaching the top-quintile range. Reducing to 5 keeps the portfolio firmly in the top-decile zone where factor theory predicts the signal is cleanest. This is not tuned to improve backtest output — it is a structural choice about *where in the momentum distribution to concentrate*.

**Expected effect:** Modestly higher stock-level alpha per position, slightly higher idiosyncratic vol per quarter. With current Sortino 9.0 there is margin to absorb this.

---

### 2. Vol-adjusted momentum in stock selection

**Files:** `features.py` (new function), `stock_model.py` (replaced call)

**Change in `features.py`:** Added `stock_momentum_12_1_voladj(px_m, vol_window=6)` alongside the original `stock_momentum_12_1`. The new function divides raw 12-1 return by 6-month trailing volatility.

**Change in `stock_model.py`:** Replaced `stock_momentum_12_1` import and call with `stock_momentum_12_1_voladj`.

**Rationale (first principles):**  
Raw 12-1 momentum ranks stocks by return magnitude, treating a +40% return achieved via erratic swings the same as a +25% steady trend. Volatility-adjusted momentum divides by trailing vol, making the score equivalent to a Sharpe ratio of recent performance. This:

- Rewards consistent, persistent trends over noisy momentum
- Pre-filters the stocks where momentum crashes concentrate (high-vol/high-momentum names, per Barroso & Santa-Clara 2015, Daniel & Moskowitz 2016)
- Does not change the lookback period, universe, or number of stocks selected — only how they are ranked

**Parameters:** `vol_window = 6` months (trailing volatility estimate). No dedicated config param added — 6 months is the standard estimate from momentum literature.

### Root cause analysis

1. **5 stocks per sector → higher concentration risk.** MaxDD got worse (-4.54% vs -3.2%) and Sortino dropped from 9.0 to 5.71. The 5th-10th momentum stocks were providing diversification benefit that outweighed the marginal signal quality gain.

2. **Vol-adjusted momentum → selects different stocks.** The new ranking deprioritizes high-vol/high-return stocks and promotes low-vol/steady-return stocks. These may be more "quality-momentum" stocks that:
   - Have weaker regime sensitivity (less beta to the HMM signal)
   - Explain why ShuffledLabels scored 95.5% — the stock selection is doing the work, not the regime

3. **Reliability 79 vs 89.** The ShuffledLabels flag directly reduces the reliability score. Everything else held.

### Decision

**Changes from Run 4 should be reverted.** Neither change improved the strategy:
- `top_n_stocks_per_sector`: revert 5 → 10
- `stock_model.py`: revert to `stock_momentum_12_1`

The vol-adjusted momentum is theoretically sound but appears to decouple the stock selection from the regime signal, which is the strategy's core edge. This is a structural incompatibility, not a parameter tuning issue.

**Run 3 remains the baseline.** No further changes until a new hypothesis is developed.
